3. User Health & Safety Notice

IMPORTANT HEALTH NOTICE: To protect our users of all ages, A Chill Day includes a mandatory 180-minute daily play time limit. After 180 minutes of active play, the game will display a mandatory rest notification.

The game is designed with a finite loop and is not intended for infinite, continuous play. We strongly encourage all users to take regular breaks and maintain a healthy balance between gaming and other activities.
